PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application (the "Application") created by Hubert Nguyen. The Application provides a convenient browser interface to assist users in looking up traffic fine information from public government portals.

IMPORTANT GOVERNMENT & DATA SOURCE DISCLAIMER

Non-Affiliation: This Application is an independent tool and is NOT affiliated with, authorized, endorsed by, or in any way officially connected with the Traffic Police Department (Cục Cảnh sát Giao thông), the Ministry of Public Security of Vietnam, or any other government entity.

Public Data Source: All traffic fine information accessed via the Application is displayed directly from official public portals, specifically csgt.vn and vnetraffic.vn. The Application acts solely as a specialized web browser utility to facilitate public access to these official sources.

1. Information Collection and Storage

A. Public Traffic Fine Lookup Data (Local Storage Only)

When you perform a lookup (such as entering a vehicle license plate number), the search is executed directly against the official public web portals.

  • On-Device Storage: Any search history or penalty details that you explicitly choose to save (e.g., via the "Save History" button) are stored EXCLUSIVELY LOCAL ON YOUR MOBILE DEVICE (using local storage/SQLite).
  • No Server Collection: We DO NOT transmit, collect, or store your vehicle license plates, search queries, or traffic fine results on any external developer servers.

B. User Provided Information

If the Application offers optional account registration or member sign-in (e.g., to manage your local garage/saved vehicles), we may receive information such as your phone number or email address solely for authentication and service provision.

C. Automatically Collected Information & Advertising

The Application may automatically collect non-personal technical information to ensure smooth operation, analytics, and advertising display, including:

  • Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
  • IP address and general coarse location data (e.g., city or zip code level).
  • App usage statistics and crash logs.

This data is processed by third-party services such as Google Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob to help analyze performance and deliver non-intrusive advertisements.

2. User Consent Mechanism (Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP Compliance)

In compliance with personal data protection regulations (including Vietnam's Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP), the Application requires explicit user consent before processing personal identifiers or lookup data. By interacting with our consent pop-up and checking the agreement box, you confirm that:

  • You are the lawful owner of the vehicle or have explicit permission from the vehicle owner to perform the lookup.
  • You agree to the collection and processing of data solely for vehicle traffic fine lookup and local history management.

3. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to outside parties. However, non-personally identifiable information may be shared with trusted third-party service providers for analytics and advertising, including:

  • Google Play Services / Apple App Store (App distribution & In-App Purchases)
  • Google AdMob (Advertising display)
  • Firebase Analytics (App analytics and crash reporting)

4. Data Deletion and User Rights

You maintain full control over your data stored within the Application:

  • Delete Local History: You can clear your saved lookup history at any time directly in the Application's "History" or "Settings" menu.
  • Opt-Out & Account Deletion: You may uninstall the Application to immediately halt all local data storage. If you have created an account, you may request full deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at hubert.nguyen.business@gmail.com.

5. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.
