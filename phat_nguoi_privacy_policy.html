PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application (the "Application") created by Hubert Nguyen. The Application operates strictly as a specialized web browser utility to assist users in easily accessing public traffic fine information.

IMPORTANT GOVERNMENT & DATA SOURCE DISCLAIMER

Non-Affiliation: This Application is an independent tool and is NOT affiliated with, authorized, endorsed by, or in any way officially connected with the Traffic Police Department (Cục Cảnh sát Giao thông), the Ministry of Public Security of Vietnam, or any other government entity.

Pure Browser Utility: The Application only provides a WebView interface connecting directly to official public portals (csgt.vn and vnetraffic.vn).

1. Information We DO NOT Collect

Because the Application functions solely as a pass-through web browser, we want to be completely transparent about the data we do NOT collect:

  • No Search Data: We DO NOT collect, read, parse, intercept, or store any license plate numbers you enter.
  • No Traffic Fine Results: We DO NOT collect, cache, or store any traffic violation records, penalty amounts, or personal images returned by the government portals.
  • No Local Database: The Application does not maintain any local history or database of your searches on your device.

All interactions, data inputs, and logins (such as VNeTraffic accounts) happen directly between your device and the official government servers.

2. Automatically Collected Information & Advertising

To maintain, improve, and monetize the Application, we may automatically collect non-personal technical information, including:

  • Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
  • IP address and general coarse location data (e.g., city or zip code level).
  • App usage statistics and crash logs.

This data is processed by third-party services such as Google Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob to help analyze app stability and deliver advertisements.

3. Third-Party Services and External Links

When you use the Application to view traffic fines, you are browsing third-party websites. Your interaction with those official portals is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms of service. Additionally, we use the following third-party services for app operation:

  • Google Play Services / Apple App Store (App distribution & In-App Purchases)
  • Google AdMob (Advertising display)
  • Firebase Analytics (App analytics and crash reporting)

4. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.
